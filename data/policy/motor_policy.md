# Meridian General Insurance — Private Motor Own-Damage and Theft Policy

## Section A — Scope and cover

### C-01 · Vehicles covered

This policy covers private cars and two-wheelers registered for private use, as described in the policy schedule. Vehicles registered as commercial, transport or public-service vehicles are outside the scope of this policy.

### C-02 · Own damage cover

The Company will indemnify the insured against loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accidental collision or overturning, fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning, or by external means, subject to the conditions and exclusions of this policy.

### C-03 · Theft cover

The Company will indemnify the insured against loss of the insured vehicle by theft, subject to the insured lodging a First Information Report with the police and to the conditions and exclusions of this policy.

---

## Section B — Limits

### C-04 · Insured Declared Value

The Insured Declared Value recorded in the policy schedule is the maximum amount payable by the Company in respect of any one claim. Where the amount claimed exceeds the Insured Declared Value, settlement is limited to the Insured Declared Value.

### C-05 · Compulsory deductible

A compulsory deductible is borne by the insured on every own-damage claim and is deducted from the amount otherwise payable. The deductible is one hundred rupees for two-wheelers of engine capacity up to and including 150 cc, two hundred rupees for two-wheelers above 150 cc, one thousand rupees for private cars of cubic capacity up to and including 1,500 cc, and two thousand rupees for private cars above 1,500 cc.

---

## Section C — Time limits

### C-06 · Intimation of a claim

The insured shall intimate the Company of any incident giving rise to a claim under this policy within 48 hours of the occurrence of that incident.

### C-07 · First Information Report in a theft claim

In the event of theft of the insured vehicle, the insured shall lodge a First Information Report with the police within 72 hours of discovering the loss, and shall submit a copy of that report to the Company.

### C-08 · Submission of documents

The insured shall submit the complete set of documents required for the claim within 30 days of intimating the claim to the Company.

---

## Section D — Required documents

### C-09 · Documents required for an own-damage claim

An own-damage claim arising from an accident requires the completed claim form, a repair estimate from a registered workshop, and the insured's written description of the incident.

### C-10 · Documents required for a theft claim

A theft claim requires the completed claim form, a copy of the First Information Report lodged with the police, and the insured's written description of the incident.

---

## Section E — Exclusions

### C-11 · Driving licence

The Company is not liable for any claim where the vehicle was being driven by a person who did not hold a driving licence valid on the date of the incident.

### C-12 · Driving under the influence

The Company is not liable for any claim arising while the person driving the vehicle was under the influence of alcohol or of any drug not taken on medical prescription.

### C-13 · Use for hire or reward

The Company is not liable for any claim arising while the insured vehicle was being used for hire or reward, for the carriage of goods for payment, or otherwise for any commercial purpose.

### C-14 · Wear, tear and consequential loss

The Company is not liable for normal wear and tear, for depreciation, for mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure or breakage, or for any consequential loss.

---

## Section F — Conditions

### C-15 · Repair before survey

Repairs commenced before the Company's surveyor has inspected the vehicle are not payable, unless the Company has given written authorisation for those repairs to proceed.

### C-16 · Relevance of the repair estimate

The repair estimate must relate to the damage reported in the claim. Parts and labour billed for damage inconsistent with the point of impact described in the claim are not payable without further justification.
